01 / The premise

The demo is the easy part.The room is where it gets real.

Reality Check puts the people shipping AI in the same room as the people living with the problem. Not to admire the demo — to put it under pressure.

Three cities. Three different realities. One rule: bring the thing that has to work on Monday.

03Cities

~100Curated seats per city

What it is

  • Working sessions, honest teardowns, and real constraints.
  • Builders, operators, and domain experts in one conversation.
  • A room of about 100 curated people, small enough that the work has to be specific.
  • A next step you can act on after the day ends.

What it isn't

  • A keynote marathon with a networking mixer attached.
  • A demo day where the hard questions stay off stage.
  • A badge-scanning exercise dressed up as community.
  • Another Slack group that goes quiet by Friday.

07 / Questions

The short
answers.

Still unsure which room fits? Write to us and tell us what you're building.

What is Reality Check?

A three-city series by noClue in November 2026, for people who build AI and people who live with the problem it is meant to solve. Each city has its own theme, format and room. The constant is the standard: real work, honest questions, useful next steps.

Who is it for?

Engineers, founders, product people and operators shipping AI inside real companies, plus domain experts who understand where the problem actually lives. If you only want to watch a keynote, this probably isn't your room.

Do I need a finished product?

No. Bring something real: a working build, a pilot that stalled, a constraint you can't get around, or a problem worth pressure-testing. In Bangalore you build a product during the day, so you bring a laptop and complete a short setup checklist instead.

How do I apply?

Pick your city and apply on its page. Delhi runs through Commudle. Bangalore and Pune run through GoAvo. Attendance is by application, so not every applicant is selected. Compare the three rooms.

How are people selected?

Your city's City Lead reads every application and chooses people for what they have built or lived through. A link to something real helps. We confirm attendance the week before and keep a short waitlist. How selection works.

Can I speak or demo?

Yes. Submit a talk through your city's form. We choose sessions on the story and the proof, not on a product being sold.

Can I partner with Reality Check?

Yes. We work with series, platform, ecosystem, community and city partners. The scope is built around what the room actually needs, not a logo on a banner. Start the conversation.

Why Delhi, Bangalore and Pune?

Each city brings a different kind of AI reality: enterprise adoption in Delhi, hands-on shipping in Bangalore, and scale pressure in Pune. Three rooms, three conversations that don't collapse into the same panel.
