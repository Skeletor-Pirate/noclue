02 / Why partner

A room that
does something.

You don't get a logo on a banner and a crowd walking past. You get engineers, founders, and domain experts working with your tools on a real problem, in front of the people who make adoption decisions.

The formats are built to produce output: working builds, live demos, candid feedback, and follow-up with the builders who keep using what they made. After each event we report what happened: attendance, what was built, and who is still building a month later.

No pre-qualified user claims. No badge scanning.
