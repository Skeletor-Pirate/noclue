01 / The belief

The future of AI won't be decided in a demo.It'll be decided in the room where it has to work.

Most AI conversations stop at what's possible. The harder, more useful conversation starts after that: what happens when the model meets a messy dataset, a security review, a sceptical operator, or a business that needs the thing to work on a Tuesday.

noClue exists to put the people who can build in the same room as the people who understand the problem — and to make the conversation specific enough to produce something. Not inspiration. Not attendance. A next step.

The problem is the brief.

Start from the constraint, not the trend. The most interesting AI work begins with something that doesn't fit neatly into a slide.

Show the work.

Demos are easy. Deployments are the story. We care about what happened after the demo ended.

What we have run so far: Devlution (683 attendees) and a community of 1,477.

Curate for friction.

Put builders, operators, and domain experts in the same conversation. Agreement is comfortable; friction is useful.

Output over attendance.

Leave with a decision, a collaborator, a fix, or a reason to stop. A folder of notes is not an outcome.

The network is the product.

The room should outlast the event. The point is not a calendar; it's a group of people who keep building together.

No invented proof.

If it hasn't happened, we don't claim it. No fake stats, no borrowed logos, no imaginary case studies. The work speaks first.
