The noClue manifesto

A few problems with how AI gets built.

We started noClue because the rooms we wanted didn't exist. The ones that did were built for announcements, not work. This is what we believe — and what we're building toward.

No. 001 India → US / EU Read time · 2 min

We're not trying to be the loudest room. We're trying to be the one people leave with something.

The AI conversation has a volume problem. Plenty of certainty, plenty of theatre, very little evidence of what happens when the system meets a real business, a real dataset, or a real Monday morning.

noClue is our attempt to build the opposite: smaller, more honest, more useful. Here is the short version of what we believe. We'll keep testing it against the work.

01

AI is not a developer topic. It's a human one.

The biggest AI decisions will be made in hospitals, banks, warehouses, and classrooms. The people who understand those places need to be in the room where the systems are designed — not consulted at the end.

02

The best products come from people who understand the problem, not just the technology.

Domain knowledge is not a nice-to-have. It is the raw material. Engineers who have never lived the problem can build something impressive and useless at the same time.

03

Demos prove ideas. Deployments prove value.

A demo survives a stage. A deployment survives a Monday. We care about the second one — the data, the cost, the failure modes, and the person who has to support it at 2 AM.

04

Small rooms beat big crowds.

A hundred people who build will outproduce a thousand who watch. We would rather curate hard and leave seats empty than fill a room with people who came for the bag.

05

What you've built matters more than where you studied.

Pedigree is a proxy. Work is evidence. We will keep choosing the person with a working thing and a clear explanation over the person with the right logo on their CV.

06

Builders deserve to be paid.

Community is not a business model. Exposure does not pay rent. The long-term shape of noClue includes paid work for the people doing it — through projects, labs, and the network itself.

07

Great ecosystems are connected across borders, not isolated by them.

India has builders. The US and Europe have problems and resources. The distance between them is mostly logistics, not talent. We're building the bridge on purpose.

08

We'd rather ship something small than announce something big.

Restraint is part of the brand. We will not promise a date we haven't validated or a product we haven't built. If it isn't real yet, we'll say so.

In short

“Less hype. More shipped. That's the whole thing.”
