01 / The series

One series.
Three different pressures.

A demo is not a deployment. A pilot is not a product. The gap between the two is where most enterprise AI quietly dies — in data pipelines, security reviews, procurement, and the small matter of making the thing useful on a Tuesday.

01 / Delhi

Delhi

Pilot to Product

Most enterprise AI doesn't fail in the demo. It dies in the pilot.

A day on the unglamorous middle: data that isn't clean, security that won't budge, procurement that takes months, and a business case that has to survive contact with finance. For the people who have to make AI work inside a real company — not just announce it.

Focus
Enterprise AI, commercial
Format
Talks, teardowns, working sessions
Room

Output
A path past the pilot

Who we invite

02 / Bangalore

Bangalore

Shipped Live

One product. One day. Built live.

A workshop-led build day for about 100 curated builders. You start with an empty folder and finish with a working product on your own laptop, which you show in a live Ship and Show.

Focus
Hands-on building
Format
Four workshops and a live demo
Room
Engineers, founders, product people
Output
A working build on your laptop

Date and place

Sunday 6 December 2026 · Paytm office. Free to attend. Lunch and refreshments provided.

How the day works

Four workshops, one product. Each workshop adds one real layer, so you see how the pieces fit, not just how one tool works on its own.

You write the prompts. You shape the look, the copy and one feature of your own, so the product is yours.

Nobody falls behind. Every step has a saved checkpoint you can jump to.

The day at a glance

9:00 AM check-in and setup. 10:00 AM opening. Hands-on workshops of up to 50 minutes each, with a lunch break. A build sprint. Ship and Show, with live demos. 4:00 PM close.

What to bring

A laptop and charger.

What you leave with

A working product on your laptop, and a place in the live demo.
