Reality Check / 2026

Three cities.
No two the same.

Reality Check is a full-day series across Delhi, Bangalore, and Mumbai, built around one idea: real-world AI use cases in enterprises. Every city has its own theme, its own format, and its own room. The same umbrella, three different experiences.

Nov 2026 Delhi · Bangalore · Mumbai ~100 per city

Most AI events talk about what's possible. Reality Check is about what's actually happening inside companies.

A demo is not a deployment. A pilot is not a product. The gap between the two is where most enterprise AI quietly dies — in data pipelines, security reviews, procurement, and the small matter of making the thing useful on a Tuesday.

Reality Check puts the people who live in that gap in one room: engineers shipping AI, founders integrating it, and the professionals responsible for AI decisions inside real companies. The conversation includes the friction. Especially the friction.

Pilot to Product

Most enterprise AI doesn't fail in the demo. It dies in the pilot.

A day on the unglamorous middle: data that isn't clean, security that won't budge, procurement that takes months, and a business case that has to survive contact with finance. For the people who have to make AI work inside a real company — not just announce it.

Shipped Live

One product. One day. Built across the tools in the room.

A workshop-led build day. Teams work across multiple partner tools to ship one working product, ending in a live Ship and Show. No imaginary architecture. No “we'll integrate it later.” You leave with something that runs — and with the people you built it with.

03 / Mumbai

Mumbai

Breaking Point

What breaks between 10 users and 10,000?

A Build vs. Scale session on where technology ends and scale bottlenecks begin. Cost, latency, evaluation, support, trust, and the organisational seams that show up the moment something real starts working. Bring the thing that's straining. Leave with a map of why.
